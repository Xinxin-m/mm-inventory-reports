66. Which MMs lose money around a taker-imbalance event

On 10,635 of the 126,978 (event x MM) rows, the MM's HYPE PnL falls at least $10,000 from its high within 2 hours of the event start. That dollar floor is the first of three proposed tests, and 1,952 rows from 169 MMs pass all three. Most of the loss is on the position the MM already held when the event began: at the trough that position accounts for a median 58% of the drawdown. Ten MMs hold 48% of the flagged rows, and 44% of flagged rows end the 2 hours with HYPE PnL at or above zero. The thresholds, the loss measure and the account-wide measure are open decisions, listed with their consequences in section 66.6.

Setup: population, sources and compute

Events. The 915 taker-imbalance events of set 1 (|zNET| ≥ 3.70 on 30-minute HYPE net taker flow), 2025-04-01 to 2026-06-30, tables/redef2_imb_events_recommended_absgate.csv. 767 events are one 30-minute bin; the longest is 180 minutes.

Rows. One row per (event x active MM), 126,978 rows over 2,203 MMs, from data/redef2_pop_parts_all_orderfix/ under the fill-based gate on every date. 7,496 rows are shocked and 119,482 unshocked.

Builders. code/loss0917_v1_read.py (per-MM, per-minute fill aggregates and a 1-minute HYPE mid from grouped_trade_oid), code/loss0917_v1_build.py (PnL paths, drawdown, trough timing, the 30-day baseline, account value from summaries_full), code/loss0917_av_*.py (account value between snapshots, section 66.4), and for this page code/loss0922_numbers.py, code/loss0922_figures.py and code/render_loss0922_report.py. The path build ran on 2026-09-17 inside the interactive allocation; the account-value build ran as a Slurm batch job on soal (32 CPUs, 256 GB, 40 minutes). Plan: plans/plan-2026-09-17-mm-loss-episodes.md. Dataset record: drafts/loss0917_av_README.md.

Every exhibit includes 2025-10-10 and 2026-01-31. No date is excluded anywhere on this page.

66.1 The loss measure and the three tests

A loss episode is one MM in one event whose HYPE PnL falls far below its high in the 2 hours after the event starts. The measure marks every position to market each minute, because an MM that still holds the inventory it bought has realized nothing: on the 1,520 rows whose HYPE PnL over 2 hours is below −$50,000, HYPE closed PnL over the same 2 hours is zero or positive on 47%.

Table 1. Symbols used on this page.

symbol meaning
t0 event start: the clock time at which the first flagged 30-minute flow bin opens, UTC
t1 event end, exclusive: the close of the last flagged bin
row one MM in one event; the MM is an MM-labeled wallet active in HYPE during the event
shocked the MM's HYPE position changes during the event by at least 3 times sig24, its own typical 24-hour position swing (never less than $12,500 of HYPE)
q0 the MM's HYPE position just before t0, in HYPE
mid(t) the last trade-time HYPE mid of minute t
s +1 for a buy fill, −1 for a sell fill
AV0 the MM's total perp account value at the last exchange snapshot before t0, at most 24 hours old
MDD max drawdown: the largest fall of the PnL path from an earlier high, USD
L0 loss from t0: the largest amount by which the PnL path falls below zero, USD
trough the minute of the low that sets MDD
own inventory peak the minute at which the MM's position is furthest from q0 inside the 2 hours
T60 the first minute after the own inventory peak at which 60% of that position move is unwound
held share the share of MDD that is the loss on q0 at the trough 
PnL(t)  = q0 x (mid(t) - mid(t0))  +  sum over fills in (t0, t] of s x size x (mid(t) - price)  -  fees,     t0 <= t <= t0 + 120 min
MDD     = max over t of [ max over u <= t of PnL(u)  -  PnL(t) ]          positive = a loss
L0      = - min over t of PnL(t)                                         positive = a loss
held    = - q0 x (mid(trough) - mid(t0)) / MDD                           1 = the whole drawdown is on the position held at t0

The first term of PnL(t) is the gain or loss on the position held at t0; the second is the gain or loss on the fills made after t0. Both loss measures are positive dollars, so a larger number is a larger loss.

The three tests

  • Test 1, dollar floor: MDD ≥ $10,000. It keeps losses large enough to matter to an MM's book.
  • Worked example: an MM long 50,000 HYPE at $40 holds $2 million of HYPE. A $0.20 fall, 0.5%, costs it $10,000.
  • It binds on small positions. The flagged rows hold 37,775 HYPE at the median; an MM holding 3,000 HYPE needs a $3.33 fall, 8.3% at $40, to reach the floor.
  • Test 2, account value: MDD ≥ 1% of AV0. It keeps losses large relative to the MM's own capital.
  • Worked example: at the median account value of the flagged rows, $5,426,963, 1% is $54,270, 5.4 times the floor.
  • It binds on large accounts, and it cannot be read before 2025-07-10, when the account feed starts.
  • Test 3, own history: MDD above the MM's own 95th percentile of MDD over the prior 30 days. It keeps losses that are unusual for that MM.
  • The baseline windows are the 2-hour windows of a fixed grid that hold at least one of the MM's fills; the MM needs 30 of them.
  • Worked example: the MM of sample A in Figure 2 had a 95th-percentile 2-hour drawdown of $46,228 over its prior 30 days and lost $50,735, so it passes.

66.3 Where the loss comes from and when

The typical flagged loss is a price move against inventory the MM held before the event started. At the trough, the position held at t0 accounts for a median 58% of the drawdown and for 93% or more on a quarter of rows. The trough comes late, 83 minutes after t0 at the median, after the event window has closed on most rows.

66.4 Account value between exchange snapshots

Test 2 and any account-wide loss need the account value at the minute of the event, while exchange snapshots of an account arrive every 2 hours or less often. The dataset data/loss0917_av/ rolls the account value forward from the latest snapshot with every fill on every coin, funding and cash flow. With hourly node snapshots as extra starting points, the rolled value lands within 0.012% of the next observed snapshot at the median and within 0.29% at p95.

W(t)   = sum over coins c of pos_c(t) x mark_c(t)  +  sum over fills before t of (-delta x px - fee)  +  sum over funding before t of amount
AV(t)  = AV(anchor) + [W(t) - W(anchor)] + [F(t) - F(anchor)]
pnl_h  = W(t0 + h) - W(t0),     h in {2 h, 6 h, 24 h}
ret_h  = pnl_h / AV(t0)

W is the trading wealth: mark-to-market on every main-site perp, fees and funding. F is the cumulative ledger cash flow into the perp account, so deposits, withdrawals and transfers move AV and leave pnl_h untouched. The anchor is the most recent Allium account read or hourly node snapshot.

66.5 Remarks that need attention

Each remark is a reading with no settled explanation yet, and each ends with the check that would settle it.

  • On 37% of flagged rows the MM's largest position move inside the 2 hours is under 5% of the position it held at t0, and on 49% under 10%. Table 7 times each trough against that move, so on these rows the own inventory peak is a small fluctuation; sample B's is 56 HYPE on a position of 22,941 HYPE. Check: classify the timing only on rows whose position move reaches 10% of q0 or the MM's sig24.
  • 44% of flagged rows end the 2 hours with HYPE PnL at or above zero, and 37% end down $10,000 or more. Max drawdown counts a fall from a high inside the 2 hours, so it flags MMs that recovered. Check: report L0 and the 2-hour PnL beside MDD (decision D1).
  • Account-wide and HYPE-only 2-hour PnL differ widely on flagged rows. Account minus HYPE is −$456,449, −$29,101, $183, $38,602, $653,883 at p5, p25, p50, p75 and p95 over 9,067 rows, and the account-wide 2-hour PnL is negative on 52.1% of them against 55.5% for HYPE alone. Check: apply test 1 to the account-wide pnl_h2 (decision D7) and compare the two flagged sets.
  • The rolled account value fits less well through 2026. The share of intervals with error above 5% rises from 0.1% in 2025-10 to 19.5% in 2026-06, concentrated in 240 wallets by June. Possible cause: W leaves out spot, builder-DEX coins and collateral held outside the perp account. Check: add node-snapshot spot balances for the flagged wallets and see whether the error collapses.
  • The closed-PnL loss flag of the Exp8 population files barely separates shocked from unshocked MMs: it fires on 27.1% of shocked rows and 23.0% of unshocked rows (114,479 rows, column pnl_below_7d_q10). The flag compares closed PnL in the event window with the MM's 10th percentile of 30-minute closed PnL over the prior 7 days, and closed PnL misses the loss on inventory still held (section 66.1). Check: the same comparison on MDD over the same window length.

66.7 Limits and data

  • HYPE only. The tests read HYPE PnL. A loss offset in another coin, in spot or on another venue still counts as a loss; the account-wide pnl_h of section 66.4 is the check.
  • Funding is left out of the 2-hour HYPE path.
  • The mid is the last trade-time mid of each minute, 1.8 bps from the book mid at the median.
  • Fill order inside a minute. grouped_trade_oid rows inside one minute are out of exchange order; on 9.6% of minutes with a fill the rebuilt position is off, by 32 HYPE at the median, about $13 of PnL on a 1% price move.
  • The fill table is incomplete before 2025-05-26 (section 62). Those dates hold 110 of the 915 events and 555 of the floor rows.
  • The account feed starts on 2025-07-10, and 2025-11-20 to 2025-11-28 has no Allium snapshots; the hybrid build bridges those nine days with node snapshots, while test 2 as run reads summaries_full and leaves 49 floor rows there without AV0.
