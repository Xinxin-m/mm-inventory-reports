Change log

What each day added to the site: new pages, new sections, and revisions to sections that already existed. Every line links to the place it describes. New results and revisions inside the window below carry a NEW tag, here and in the side menu, where they are also set in a brighter blue.

The choice is kept in this browser and applies to the side menu on every page. 0d is today only, by your own calendar. New results and revisions count; pages split out of sections that were already published do not.

How the log is kept

The site builder compares the section list of every page against what it has already recorded, and writes any new page or section under the build date. A revision to an existing section leaves the section list unchanged, so those lines are written by hand into code/site_changelog.json. Days before 2026-09-17 were rebuilt from the git history of the published site, one snapshot per day. A section that moves to another page keeps its entry, and the link follows it.
