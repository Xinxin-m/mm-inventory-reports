Change log

What each day added to the site: new pages, new sections, and revisions to sections that already existed. Every line links to the place it describes. New results and revisions on or after the day set below carry a [NEW] tag, here and in the side menu, where they are also set in blue.

09-2509-2409-23

The choice is kept in this browser and applies to the side menu on every page. By default only results dated 2026-09-23 or later are new, and nothing on the taker-imbalance page is, whatever its date. New results count; wording-only revisions and pages split out of sections that were already published do not.

How the log is kept

The site builder compares the section list of every page against what it has already recorded, and writes any new page or section under the build date. A revision to an existing section leaves the section list unchanged, so those lines are written by hand into code/site_changelog.json. Days before 2026-09-17 were rebuilt from the git history of the published site, one snapshot per day. A section that moves to another page keeps its entry, and the link follows it.
