Plan: wrapping up the one-sided surge study

The plan that turns the one-sided surge results into one wrap-up page, box by box along the flow chart.

  • Question. When MMs respond to a one-sided flow shock, does their response stabilize the market or amplify the disruption?
  • Event set. The 922 one-sided surges.
  • Active MMs. The order-rate population (A1): MM-labeled on the date, and HYPE limit orders at an average of at least 1 per minute in the 2 hours before the surge or during it. It replaces the fill-rule population, which also needed a fill before and during the surge and a post-only fill before it.
  • Absorbers. K90 (A2): the smallest set of those MMs that holds 90% of the maker volume on the side the surge takers hit.
  • Status on 2026-09-24. A1 and A2 are tested and decided. Sections A5 to K are next; B, D, H and J are reductions of tables that exist, and C, E and I each need one Slurm job.
  • The deliverable. One new page following the flow chart: events and classes, who absorbs, quoting when hit, peak and unwind, the MM across surges, market cost and recovery, and what goes with recovery (Section K).

0. The question, and the rule that keeps it honest

Question (proposal). When MMs respond to a one-sided flow shock, does their response stabilize the market or amplify the disruption? The study follows the flow chart:

flow shock  ->  trade (who absorbs)  ->  inventory shock (who is shocked)
            ->  MM action (where they quote, what they cancel, whether they cross)
            ->  MM outcome (when the position peaks, how fast it comes off, what it cost)
            ->  market outcome (spread, depth, taker cost, time to recover)
            ->  price outcome (does the move revert)

Every quantity carries one of four tags.

tag meaning examples
S state before the shock the MM before t0 PRE2H volume, pre-event inventory, sig24, leverage, two-sidedness, order rate
A action what the MM chose placement distance per side, resting size per side, cancels, new opens, own taker trades
O MM outcome its position and book t*, A*, T60, held share, markout, PnL, equity
C consequence for the market the market spread, depth, taker cost, recovery time, R(120)
  • An exhibit explains an outcome only by a state or an action. The plan tests A -> O inside the MM and O -> C across MMs.
  • Unwinding early or fast is an outcome (O). It follows from tilting (A) and from the flow moving on, so "early unwinders tilt more" is expected. The finding is how MMs get out (exit-side maker fills, own taker trades, or the flow reversing) and what that costs them and the market.
Already built and reused
  • The catalogue with the five anchors (t0, t_pv, t_c, t1, t_pp) and the three classes (561 / 314 / 47).
  • The per-surge per-MM record table: absorption USD and rank; PRE2H / PRE30 / event / post volume by side and role; pre-event position; sig24; shocked_loose; order rates; per-window quote state (q_*).
  • The F1 unwind programme (draft section 72, f1u0922_*): t*, A*, T60, speed and timing classes, minute-bucket quote state on the MM's own clock, 31,350 accumulator (MM, surge) pairs.
  • place0922_* (placement-time distance), f1r0921_* (spread / depth / taker-cost phase panel), tick0922* (tick decomposition), rvb0922_* (reversal quintiles), casc0922_* (HYPE cascade episodes), data/loss0917_av/ (5-minute account value), the Exp11 per-MM profiles.
  • Still to build: the event-clock quote state with markouts (E), the liquidity panel on both sides (I), and the tick-level ladders at absorption fills (C). The rest is reduction and relabelling.

A1. Active MM: the order-rate population

  • Fill-rule population (population of record 2026-09-22 to 2026-09-23). An (MM, surge) pair is in it when all five conditions hold:
  • ① MM label: label_v5d == 0 on HYPE on the surge's date, minus the clause-(d) relabel days.
  • ② Fill gate: at least 1 HYPE fill in PRE2H = t0 - 120 min, t0) and at least 1 in the run [t0, t1).
  • ③ Post-only rule: at least 1 post-only (ALO) fill in PRE2H.
  • ④ Order floor: at least 120 HYPE limit orders in PRE2H, or at least length_min limit orders in [t0, t1); that is, an average of 1 order per minute in either window. Orders are distinct limit order ids, TP/SL excluded, rejected orders dropped.
  • ⑤ Readable order store on the date.
  • Clause-(d) relabel. 306 wallets are MM in the v5d label only through clause (d) (at least 5 active days in 14, post-only share at least 0.8, mean shortest gap between orders at most 1 s, no volume floor) or through the label's second pass (a previous-day MM kept through a quiet day). They are small automated post-only traders that quote one side at a time. Their 3,028 HYPE MM days count as algo (decided 2026-09-22).
  • Order-rate population (population of record from 2026-09-23) keeps ① and ④ and drops the fill gate ② and the post-only rule ③. It holds a median 139 MMs per surge, against 100 in the fill-rule population.
  • k90, post-only. Rank every wallet, with no label or floor, by post-only maker USD in [t0, t1) on both sides. k90 is the smallest top set holding 90% of the total, and k95 the same at 95%.
Result: the fill gate ② is the condition of the fill-rule population that drops heavy quoters; the order floor ④ keeps MMs that pause. The order-rate population is adopted. (analysis 76.2)

Tested on 30 random dates including 2025-10-10, then on all 922 surges. Tables show all 922. Code Exp8/code/wrap0924_a1a2_test.py, tables Exp8/tables/wrap0924_a1_*.

  • The order floor ④ counts orders over a whole window, so MMs that pause stay in the fill-rule population. 2.8% of the fill-rule population's (MM, surge) pairs quote at 1 or more orders a minute in PRE2H and below that in the run. On 2025-10-10 that is 51 of 368 pairs.
  • The post-only k90 set is almost entirely MMs of the fill-rule population. 28 of its median 30 wallets are in the fill-rule population. The other 2 hold 1.5% of post-only maker USD at the median surge.
  • Of the 26,839 (MM, surge) pairs in k90, 5.9% fall outside the fill-rule population. The largest causes are the fill gate ② (2.6% of pairs) and the MM label ① (2.7%). The order floor ④ accounts for 0.4%.
  • The fill gate ② removes MMs that quote heavily in the run. 643 of its 691 excluded pairs place at least 1 order a minute in [t0, t1). These MMs start or resume quoting at t0. The order-rate population keeps them.
  • The order floor ④ removes slow quoters: a median 0.17 orders a minute in PRE2H and 0.43 in the run.

Summary. At the median surge, MMs of the order-rate population hold 97.9% of the post-only maker volume in [t0, t1) and 67.3% of all maker volume, and absorb 63.1% of the surge. The surge-side flow therefore meets two kinds of resting order. About two thirds are MM post-only quotes. About one third are resting orders from other wallets (algo, retail or unlabelled), almost all Gtc or other non-post-only order types. Changing the MM population moves the absorbed share by at most 6 points at the median. The all-maker k90 reaches 89% by adding those other wallets.

  • LBL = every wallet with the MM label minus the clause-(d) relabel, with no floor.
  • The window choice moves the medians by at most 0.8 points ([t0, t_c) against [t0, t1)).

Test and decision suggestion, as proposed on 2026-09-23
  • Proposal: keep the fill-rule population as the universe and add k90 as a descriptive "core MM" layer.
  • Test: size of k90 and k95, their share of the fill-rule population's count, and how many of its MMs sit outside k90.
  • Decision rule: if the median k90 has at most 25 wallets and covers under 40% of the fill-rule population's count, k90 becomes the core-MM subset for Sections C and F.

A2. Absorber: K90 on absorbed volume

abs_i   = maker HYPE of MM i on the absorption side in [t0, t_c)
unl_i   = maker HYPE of MM i on the exit side in [t0, t_c)        (reported beside abs_i, with the net abs_i - unl_i)
share_i = abs_i / sum over the surge's MMs in the order-rate population of abs_j
K90     = the smallest top set of MMs by share_i whose shares sum to 0.90
S2      = MMs with share_i >= 0.02  (the Exp10 rule; robustness column)
  • Absorption side: the side the surge takers hit (asks on a buy surge, bids on a sell surge). Every maker fill there trades against the surge flow.
  • Decided 2026-09-23: K90 ranks by absorption-side volume inside the order-rate population, and S2 is the robustness column. K90 holds a median 27 MMs per surge. That fails the proposed size cut (median at most 15), and K90 is kept by decision.
Result: inside the order-rate population, K90 on absorption-side volume and K90 on both-side volume pick 26 of about 27 same MMs; the absorption-side ranking holds more net absorption. (analysis 76.6)

Tested on 30 random dates including 2025-10-10, then on all 922 surges; tables show all 922. The test uses stored USD in [t0, t1). The exact [t0, t_c) in HYPE comes with the Section E fill pass. Code Exp8/code/wrap0924_a2b_k90_sides.py, tables Exp8/tables/wrap0924_a2b_*.

  • Absorption-side and both-side name the two rankings: by abs_i, and by abs_i + unl_i.
  • The two rankings pick the same MMs. At the median surge they share 25 of 27 wallets (Jaccard 0.87) and 4 of the top 5. Each set holds about 90% of the other's volume.
  • The 2 MMs in only one set differ in kind. The absorption-side-only MMs fill almost entirely on the absorption side (median 100% of their maker volume). The both-side-only MMs trade about half on each side (median 49%) and hold a median 0.0% of net absorption.
  • The absorption-side set therefore holds more net absorption: 92.1% against 89.3% at the median surge.
  • Adopted: K90 on absorption-side volume as the absorber rule and S2 as the robustness column (13 MMs, 74% of absorption). The both-side K90 is the candidate core-MM set.
  • Size cut: K90 holds a median 27 and a p95 of 41 MMs, above the "median at most 15, p95 at most 40" of the decision rule. S2 meets the size cut (13 and 17) and covers 74% of absorption. At 90% coverage the absorber set is about 27 MMs.
  • Both sets hold MMs that net toward the exit side during the run, a median 2 in the absorption-side set and 4 in the both-side set. unl_i records this.

G. Inventory churn as an MM characteristic (low priority, reduce-only)

Proposal: add kappa, its half-life and turnover to the Exp11 profile table (inventory_stats_top100.csv), and use the half-life once in F as an S variable: does an MM with a short normal half-life also unwind shock inventory faster?

Measures from the literature
  • Mean reversion and half-life: q_t - q_{t-1} = -kappa * q_{t-1} + e_t on the deviation from the MM's own running median, 1- or 5-minute grain; half-life ln 2 / kappa. Madhavan and Smidt (1993), Hasbrouck and Sofianos (1993), Menkveld (2013), Kirilenko, Kyle, Samadi and Tuzun (2017).
  • Inventory variability against volume: standard deviation of inventory over daily traded size; Menkveld (2013), Brogaard, Hendershott and Riordan (2014).
  • Turnover: traded size over mean absolute inventory per day.
  • Time inside a band: share of minutes with |q - median| <= 1 sig24, and how long a 2-sigma excursion lasts (the unconditional twin of T60).
  • End-of-period flatness: |q_end| / sig24 at UTC midnight.
