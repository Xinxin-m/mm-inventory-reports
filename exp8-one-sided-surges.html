Exp8 one-sided taker surges: what a side rule sees that the net rule does not

A second event definition. Each side of the taker flow gets its own score, and an event is a half hour in which one side surges (zB ≥ 3.81 for buys, zS ≥ 3.89 for sells) and dominates. That gives 922 side surges (family F1), tuned to match the 915 net-flow events in count. The section compares the two rules, times each surge on a 1-minute run, and reruns the section 4 population statistics on F1.

Key results

The two rules disagree about which half hours matter§9.2 →The 30-minute bin label is not the event§9.3 →Price: the surge almost never runs against the move, and a third of big moves come back§9.4 →

Section, table and figure numbers are those of the single Exp8 record, so they do not start at 1 here. Definitions shared by every section are in the notation and event-set sections of the main page.

9. Taker-surge event families: what a one-sided rule sees that the net rule does not

Event set 3: 922 side surges (F1), with 399 two-sided events (F2) and the 327 F1 events set 1 never selects (F3), against the 915 net-flow events of set 1. Selection is on 30-minute boxes; start ts and end te come from the 1-minute run, and the peak tc is the minute of largest cumulative net flow. Overlap with the 181 roster shocks is counted in §9.2.

Selecting on a surge in one taker side, at the same number of events as the net-imbalance rule of §3, keeps 595 of the catalogue's boxes and adds 327 new ones. The added events are half hours where both sides ran hot and the net stayed small, and they almost never contain a roster MM inventory shock: 8 of the 181, against 87 for the overlapping half. The three family catalogues are built; the MM outcome pass on them is not run.

Population and grain
  • Population. 1,730 target events over 2025-03-27 to 2026-06-30: 595 BOTH, 327 NEW_ONLY, 330 ZNET_ONLY, 478 TWO_SIDED. Every exhibit includes 2025-10-10 and 2026-01-31.
  • Grain. One row is one event. Quantiles are p5 / p25 / p50 / p75 / p95 over events.

9.1 Definitions

The side-surge score, the four sets, the three families, the duration run and the 1-minute anchors
  • Side-surge score. zB and zS are the §3 standardized scores computed on taker buy USD and taker sell USD separately, in place of the net measure zNET. The cutoff is set at the quantile that matches the catalogue's event count, q=0.9425, giving zB≥3.81 and zS≥3.89.
  • Dominance. A surging side is dominant when it carries at least half the bin’s taker USD and at least twice the other side’s USD in the previous bin. A 30-minute box is one-sided when a surging side is dominant, and two-sided when a side surged and no side is dominant.
  • The four sets. BOTH (595) are one-sided boxes that overlap an event of the §3 catalogue. NEW_ONLY (327) are one-sided boxes that overlap none. ZNET_ONLY (330) are §3 events no side-surge box touches. TWO_SIDED (478) are the two-sided boxes at the same cutoffs.
  • The three families. F1 = BOTH + NEW_ONLY, the 922 one-sided side-surge events. F2 = the 399 two-sided events with |I|<1/3 at the peak bin. F3 = NEW_ONLY, the 327 events the net rule never sees.
  • Duration run D, at 5 minutes: the maximal run of qualifying 5-minute bins around the box, one non-qualifying bin allowed inside, containing the window's largest same-side bin. A bin qualifies when its side score is in the full-sample top 10% at 5 minutes and the side is at least half the bin's taker USD.
  • The 1-minute anchors. Nt is in-direction net taker USD in minute t, and p95 is the 95th percentile of N over the 6 hours before the window. The run is the set of minutes with Nt>0.25×p95, gaps of up to 3 minutes merged, and it qualifies when some minute reaches 3×p95. ts is its first minute and te its last; tVp is the minute of largest same-side USD; tc is the minute at which cumulative N over the run is largest.
  • Price. ps is the mid at the first tick of ts. Mpeak is the largest in-direction mid move in bps from ps over ts to te+15 minutes, Mfinal is the move at the last mid of te+15, and ρ=Mfinal/Mpeak.
  • Big-move gate g: the full-sample 95th percentile of Mpeak for the event's length class, 153 bps for runs of 5 to 15 minutes, 195, 240 and 320 bps for the longer classes.

  • Roster inventory shocks live where the two rules agree.
    87 of the 181 shocks sit inside a BOTH box against 8 inside a NEW_ONLY box, and per bin flagged BOTH is 8.1 times random against 1.6 for NEW_ONLY. A one-sided surge that the other side answered rarely leaves a roster MM holding inventory.
  • The added events are two-way half hours, not quiet ones.
    NEW_ONLY carries $22.0m of taker USD, close to BOTH's $28.6m, but its median |imbalance| is 0.15 against 0.35 and its median box move is 91 bps against 156. Both sides ran hot in 88.7% of them.
  • The net rule keeps selecting boxes with no dominant side.
    ZNET_ONLY has a side surge at the matched cutoff in only 47.3% of its boxes and a dominance-passing bin in 38.2%, and it is the smallest set by taker USD.
  • TWO_SIDED events are the largest and the longest.
    Median $30.1m of taker USD, p95 $135.8m, and 36.0% run past one 30-minute bin.

  • The flow starts a median 8 minutes into the labelled half hour and ends a median 23 minutes in.
    22% of one-sided events start before the label and 21% end after it, so the bin boundary cuts the episode in roughly one event in five.
  • Pressure keeps building after the sharpest minute.
    tc sits a median 4 minutes after tVp, p95 21 minutes, and by the sharpest minute a median 79% of the run's cumulative net flow is already in. tc and te share the same median offset, so in most events the pressure peak is the end of the run.
  • 74% of runs end because the other side takes over
    , 20% because flow dries up, and 6% because the search window ran out. 3.5% of one-sided events have no 1-minute onset at all and fall back to the 5-minute run.
  • The two-sided episodes are not bursts.
    Their runs are a median 30 minutes long, start a median 14 minutes before the bin label, and sit a median 19 minutes before the roster wallet begins to form its position, against 2 minutes for one-sided events. Treating them as a surge at a point in time is not supported.

    Direction is close to guaranteed once the move is big enough to clear the gate.
  • Reversion is four times as common in the side-surge families as in the net-only set

9.6 The one-sided family F1, as implemented

Side scores, the one-sided class, the 30-minute box, the 5-minute duration run, the 1-minute window, and how F2 and F3 differ

F1: one-sided side-surge events, as built by code/redef4_events.py (stage sel30) and code/redef4_steps.py (stages targets, dur5, anchor, catalog)

  • Taker flow per bin. Bt and St are HYPE taker buy and taker sell USD in 30-minute bin t (data/redef_imb_measures_30m.parquet). For side a, Vat is that side’s USD and Vot the other side’s.
  • Side score. zat=(Vat−mat)/max(1.4826 MADat, 25000), where mat and MADat are the median and median absolute deviation of Va over the 336 bins (7 days) strictly before t, with at least 40% of them observed. The $25,000 floor binds in 0 of 21,923 valid bins per side.
  • Surge flag. Aat=1 when zat≥ca. The cutoffs are the full-sample 94.25% quantile of each side’s score, cB=3.81 and cS=3.89. The quantile 0.9425 is the one that gives as many one-sided events (922) as the net-flow catalogue has (915).
  • One-sided bin. Side a is one-sided in bin t when Aat=1, Vat≥Vot and Vat≥2 Vot−1: the surging side carries at least half of the bin’s taker USD and at least twice what the other side traded in the previous bin. A bin with a surge on some side and no one-sided side is two-sided.
  • 30-minute box. Flagged bins of the same class (one-sided buy, one-sided sell) are merged into a run, with one unflagged bin allowed between two flagged ones. The box is the run’s span; its peak bin is the bin with the largest surging-side score. F1 is every one-sided box: 922 events, 439 buy and 483 sell, on 349 dates, 3 of them on 2025-10-10.
  • Duration run D, 5 minutes. Search window: the box plus one 30-minute bin on each side. A 5-minute bin qualifies when its side score (same construction on 5-minute bins, 2,016-bin trailing window) is at or above the full-sample 90% quantile, 3.33 for buys and 3.51 for sells, and Va≥Vo in the bin. D is the run of qualifying bins, one non-qualifying bin allowed inside, that contains the window’s largest same-side bin; if none contains it, the run with the most same-side USD.
  • 1-minute window. Search window: D plus 5 minutes on each side. Nt=Vat−Vot is in-direction net taker USD in minute t; p95 is the 95th percentile of N over the 360 minutes before the search window, floored at $5,000. A run is a stretch of minutes with Nt>0.25×p95, with gaps of up to 3 minutes merged. A run qualifies when one of its minutes has Nt≥3×p95; if no run does, the bar drops to 2×p95 (63 events). The primary run is the qualifying run with the largest ∑Nt. ts is its first minute and te its last. If no run qualifies at either bar (32 events), ts and te are the first and last minutes of D.
  • Event window used by the population build. [t0,t1)=[ts, te+1 min): median 14 minutes, p5 4, p25 9, p75 23, p95 38. peak_utc is tc, the minute at which cumulative N from ts is largest. The event side (hit_side) is the surging side a; the absorption side is the maker side opposite it.
  • Sets inside F1. BOTH (595): the box overlaps one of the 915 net-flow 30-minute boxes. NEW_ONLY (327): it overlaps none.
  • Classes. class3 in the population tables is the absolute price gate, as for the net-flow catalogue: liquidation cascade when forced taker USD is at least 10% of the box, otherwise big move when the box start-to-end mid move is at least 168.36 bps; F1 has 47 cascade, 314 big-move and 561 other events.

For contrast: the net-flow catalogue, F2 and F3

  • Net-flow catalogue (§3, 915 events). The same 7-day robust score computed on net taker USD Bt−St in 30-minute bins, flagged when |zNET|≥3.70, the full-sample 95% quantile. Its population windows are the 30-minute boxes (median 30 minutes, up to 180).
  • F2, two-sided (399 events). Boxes of two-sided bins at the same cutoffs (some side surged, no side passed the one-sided test), kept when |I|<1/3 at the peak bin, I=(B−S)/(B+S); 79 of 478 are dropped by that test. The 5-minute run uses a score on total taker USD B+S (cutoff 3.06) with no side condition. The 1-minute run uses the excess Xt=(Bt+St)−median6h(B+S) in place of Nt, with the same 3, 2 and 0.25 multiples of its 6-hour p95, and the direction is the sign of the run’s cumulative net flow. On all 478 two-sided candidates these runs are long: median 30 minutes, p95 96.
  • F3, side-only (327 events). The NEW_ONLY part of F1: one-sided boxes that overlap no net-flow event. Same windows as in F1.
