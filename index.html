#AskStateWhere in the reportWhat it says, and what is missing
1The exact equation for standardising inventory. Is it a z-score? What is the divisor? DONE The exact construction (directly below Table 1) · §A Definitions A robust z-score: dev = position − trailing 6-hour median; z = dev / σt, with σt = max(7-day MAD of dev, 2% of the 30-day p95 |position|, $12,500 / mid). Candidate at |z| ≥ 6. Each of the ten rules is one bullet, in funnel order.
2Why the window changed from 6 hours to 7 days; the equation. DONE The exact construction · B2, “Baseline” row · J1, the two floors Both windows are used: the 6-hour median centres the position, the 7-day MAD scales it. The report states this; it does not yet say in one sentence why the scale is not also 6 hours (a 6-hour spread would contain the shock and shrink the yardstick while the shock forms).
3Descriptive-statistics table (N, mean, std, p25, median, p75, min, max) for inventory, standardised inventory, shock size, taker imbalance, equity. DONE Table 2 (panels A–E) · Table 3 (shock size vs pre-shock level, own volume) · Table 4 (in-shock vs all times) · Table 5 (equity and leverage around shocks) · J2 per wallet Complete. Inventory is reported as exposure |P| in kUSD so long and short bins do not cancel; the equity panel is cross-margin perp account value, ten roster MMs, one value per wallet-day, 2025-09-27 onward.
4Do shocks move equity or margin? What share of shocks cause a large equity drop? DONE at hours resolution §F Shock atlas · Table 21 · Table 5 Of 138 covered shocks, 20.3% are followed by an equity drop worse than −1% and 7.2% worse than −5% (17.4% / 4.5% without 2025-10-10). Maintenance over equity rises by more than 2 pp in 13.1% and stays below 0.20 in 137 of 140. Snapshots are ~2 h apart, so this is an hours claim, never a 5-minute one. Missing: the link to Miao’s equity and portfolio shocks; no shared event grain exists yet.
5The observed chain: price drops, takers sell, MM inventory rises, MMs unwind with limit orders. DONE (confirmed on 181) §C and Table 10 (flow) · §F (price against the inventory) · §E2 and Table 18 (post-only unwind) Hit-side taker volume 5.3× its 24-hour rate, imbalance +0.44; the mid fell in 115 of 125 up shocks, median formation move −85 bps; unwinding is 98% post-only at the median for the roster ten. Post-only unwinding is a property of these ten, not of shocked MMs generally (§E2). Extended 2026-09-04 to all 1,964 shocked-MM rows (§D4, full page): the typical shocked MM of any role does not take more than its own normal after the peak; what the roster takes is two thirds on the unwind side; order counts tilt only one point; the unwind is carried by reducing-side filled orders (unwind-side share 0.51 → 0.61, above one half in 91% of catalogue rows).
6After a shock, measure quoting, not fills: net posted and net withdrawn liquidity at and away from the touch, 5-minute steps for 2 hours, against four predicted signs. DONE on all 181 shocks (published 2026-09-04) §G Book response · Table 22 (before / during / after) · Table 23 (the four predicted signs) · Figures 14–17 The shocked MM moves where it quotes, not how much: the accumulating-side best quote backs off by 1.36 bps in 81% of shocks, depth stays balanced. Sign 1 (post more at the unwind touch) confirms, 70% of 180. Sign 2 (withdraw more from the accumulating touch) comes out opposite because these desks re-quote every ~1.3 s, so cancels travel with posts. Sign 3 (post less away) fails at 25, 50 and 100 bps. Sign 4 (withdrawal away ≥ 0) confirms. Grain is 1 minute, not 5, because the median formation is 20 minutes. Two design choices remain open: the touch band and where “away” starts.
