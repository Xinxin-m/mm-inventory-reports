The taker-surge storyline →

One flow chart in the grammar of the draft paper's Figure 1. A surge of very large taker orders moves the price and sweeps the resting orders. Nearly every MM absorbs some, and the inventory lands on a few large, busy MMs. They back the absorbing quote off and unwind passively, at a speed that is a trait of the MM. The equity effect is small. Every number carries its event set and the experiment behind it.

The story so far →

Five observation threads with the report's figures, the candidate story in one paragraph, the six tests that would confirm or break it, the order of work, the limits, and a 15-minute running order. Written on the 181 roster shocks. The storyline page is its successor on the market-flow events.

How to read these. Everything here is a descriptive association; no causal claim is made. The two studies use different event definitions and should not be pooled. The 2026-09-04 report detects events on ten wallets, and its counts describe those ten. The redefinition page detects events on market-wide taker flow and reads the whole labelled MM population against them. Each page carries its own limits section and reads an analysis.md that is the authoritative record. Numbers that changed during review stay in the reports with the reason.
