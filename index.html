Post-only unwinding is a property of these ten, not of shocked MMs generally (§E2).
6After a shock, measure quoting, not fills: net posted and net withdrawn liquidity at and away from the touch, 5-minute steps for 2 hours, against four predicted signs. DONE on all 181 shocks (published 2026-09-04) §G Book response · Table 22 (before / during / after) · Table 23 (the four predicted signs) · Figures 14–17 The shocked MM moves where it quotes, not how much: the accumulating-side best quote backs off by 1.36 bps in 81% of shocks, depth stays balanced. Sign 1 (post more at the unwind touch) confirms, 70% of 180. Sign 2 (withdraw more from the accumulating touch) comes out opposite because these desks re-quote every ~1.3 s, so cancels travel with posts. Sign 3 (post less away) fails at 25, 50 and 100 bps. Sign 4 (withdrawal away ≥ 0) confirms. Grain is 1 minute, not 5, because the median formation is 20 minutes. Two design choices remain open: the touch band and where “away” starts.
