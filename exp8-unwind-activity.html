D4. Do shocked MMs take, or quote, their way out? NEW

Every shocked market maker in every one of the 181 shocks — the catalogue wallet and every other MM that was shocked alongside it — measured in the two hours after its own inventory peak against its own pre-shock normal: how much it crosses the spread, which side it crosses on, how many orders it submits and cancels and on which side, and where the unwind actually shows up.

Four things this page says. (1) Shocked MMs do not take more than usual. For the typical shocked wallet, taker (spread-crossing) volume in the two hours after its peak is about its baseline rate: median ratio 0.91 for the catalogue wallet, 0.87 for shocked roster peers, 1.40 for shocked non-roster MMs. Its share of own volume done as taker does not move (about 2% before and after). The median 5-minute bin has no taker fill at all, before or after. (2) What little is taken is on the unwind side for the roster: two thirds of the catalogue wallet's post-peak taker dollars reduce the position (baseline one half), three quarters in the first 30 minutes. Non-roster MMs barely tilt (55%). (3) Order counts rise about 30% and tilt only one percentage point toward the unwind side over two hours; the tilt is four times larger in the first 30 minutes and gone after two hours. Cancels tilt exactly with submits, because these desks re-quote continuously. (4) The unwind is in the filled orders. Reducing-side filled orders per bin rise from 9.4 to 15.7 for the catalogue wallet while the other side stays at 9; the unwind-side share of filled orders goes from 0.51 to 0.61 and is above one half in 91% of catalogue rows, 81% of roster peers, 67% of non-roster MMs. Every role unwinds through resting-order fills on the reducing side, and the roster does it hardest.
What this adds to the report. §E2 and Table 18 give the taker share of the roster's unwind (median 2%) and §G gives the roster's quote placement from the raw order stream. Neither compares a wallet with its own pre-shock normal, and neither covers the shocked MMs outside the ten. This page does both, from the dense 5-minute panel, and answers the two items on the D3 review list that were still open: do shocked MMs take at all after the peak, and do they take more than usual in the reducing direction.

Definitions

Population
The 2,022 shock × wallet rows behind §E1 (the catalogue wallet, every roster peer and every labelled non-roster MM with a standardised inventory move of at least 3 in the shock's window), each with its own peak bin and its own unwind side. 1,964 are inside the 1,035-wallet panel cohort and are used here; the 58 dropped are all non-roster wallets.
Unwind side and shock side
Every quantity is computed per side and named by its relation to the shock, never as bid or ask. The shock side is the side whose fills built the position: buying (bids) after an up shock, selling (asks) after a down shock. The unwind side is the opposite one, the side that reduces the position: selling after an up shock, buying after a down shock. Every "unwind-side share" is unwind ÷ (unwind + shock side): 0.5 = no tilt, 1 = everything on the unwind side. Up and down shocks pool on this convention and overlay when split (paired submit tilt +0.010 for up shocks, +0.009 for down).
Windows
On the 5-minute grid, a bin labelled b covers [b, b+5 min). With t0 the shock's formation start and pk the wallet's own peak bin (its end-of-bin position is the peak): BASE = t0 − 24 h to t0 − 2 h (264 bins, the wallet's own normal); PRE = the 2 h before t0; FORM = t0 to pk, the build; POST1 = the 30 min after pk; POST = the 2 h after pk (24 bins, the headline); TAIL = 2 h to 6 h after pk.
Rate, ratio, active-bin median
A rate is the window total divided by its number of bins, so it is per 5 minutes and counts quiet bins as zero. The ratio POST ÷ BASE is computed per wallet and is undefined when the BASE rate is zero (stated wherever it bites). The active-bin median is the median over bins with a fill or an order event; for taker volume it is zero in every role, which is why the comparison is made on rates and pooled dollars rather than on medians.
Taker share of own volume
Taker USD ÷ (taker + maker USD) of the wallet's own fills in the window.
Submits, cancels, rejects, filled orders
Counts of orders per bin and side from the panel (no order notional exists there; posted USD is in §G for the catalogue wallets only). Submits count every placed order, including ones the exchange rejected (a post-only order that would have crossed, or a margin rejection). A partially filled resting order is normally cancelled for its remainder, so it appears in both counts. Hence submits − cancels is not a depth change: net of rejects it sums to zero over the two hours, and what it picks up is the reject count. Filled orders = orders with at least one fill in the bin, by wallet side.
Paired statistics
For each wallet the POST value minus its own BASE value; then the median across wallets. Quantiles are reported as p25 / p50 / p75.

  • Counts tilt to the unwind side by about one percentage point over the two hours: 50.8% of submits against 50.0% in the baseline. Real and small; four times larger in the first 30 minutes for the catalogue wallet (53.1%); gone in TAIL. The pooled event-time curve (Figure 3a below) puts the catalogue wallet's unwind-side share of submits at 0.65 in the peak bin, 0.73 at +5 min, 0.60 at +20, 0.53 at +45 and 0.50 by +60 min.
  • Cancels tilt exactly with submits because these desks re-quote continuously: an unwind-side post is followed by an unwind-side cancel within the bin. This is the fact behind §G's "wrong-sign" cancel cell.
  • Submits minus cancels is a reject count. It is about 1% of submits and, net of rejects, sums to zero over POST. Its unwind-side tilt is the tilt of post-only orders the exchange refused because they would have crossed: the unwind-side share of rejects rises from 0.54 to 0.63 for the catalogue wallet (77% of rows), 0.53 to 0.65 for roster peers, 0.52 to 0.55 for non-roster MMs. A rejected post-only order on the unwind side is an attempt to quote at or through the opposite best — the count-level trace of the quote-distance tilt §G measures in basis points.

This is the cleanest statement on the page. Submit counts barely tilt, taker volume barely moves, but the orders that actually get filled shift to the reducing side in every role — for the catalogue wallet the reducing-side fill count rises by two thirds while the other side is flat. Shocked MMs of every role unwind through resting-order fills on the reducing side; the roster does it hardest, and the non-roster MMs, which get filled on both sides more often after the peak, least.

6. Validation

  • 2,022 rows in, 1,964 kept; the 58 dropped are all non-roster wallets outside the panel cohort. (shock, wallet, window) is unique.
  • Every row has 24 POST, 24 PRE, 6 POST1 and 48 TAIL bins on the dense grid; four rows on the panel's first date have 248 of 264 BASE bins. Order columns are present in 100% of POST bins of every row (217 panel dates read, 35 from the 2026-04-16 onward sidecar).
  • Independent fill reconciliation. Over the 12 bins covering fills in [peak, peak + 60 min) the panel's reducing-side USD (maker + taker) matches the §E rollout's 60-minute figure, built separately from the grouped-trade feed, within 1% on 1,764 of 1,764 rows with a non-zero value (median relative difference 0.0000), and the taker share within 0.02 on all of them. The naive alignment that starts one bin later matches on only 23%: the peak bin's fills belong to the build (the 5-minute position is end-of-bin), and the rollout's 60-minute window on fill timestamps includes them, so §E's 60-minute quantities are [peak, +60 min) in bin terms, one bin different from a strictly post-peak window.
  • Symmetry null: BASE unwind-side share medians 0.4997 (submits), 0.4991 (cancels), 0.504 (taker USD), 0.501 (maker USD).
  • Net of rejects, Σ(submits − cancels − rejects) over POST is −55k on the unwind side against 618k filled orders, which is why "net open minus cancel" is read as a reject count and the unwind is read off filled-order counts.

7. Not established

  • Order notional by side for the population: the panel has counts only. Posted and cancelled USD by distance bucket exist in §G for the catalogue wallets.
  • Whether the shock-side taker fills in the peak bin are voluntary, forced (liquidation / ADL) or a hedge leg: the panel's liquidation and ADL columns were not joined.
  • Any causal reading. The post-peak tilt is consistent with inventory management and with the same directional flow that built the position continuing to hit the other side of the book; the one-sided surges of §C apply.
  • The 58 non-roster rows outside the panel cohort.
