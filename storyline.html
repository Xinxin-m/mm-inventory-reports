How a taker-flow surge moves through the HYPE market makers

Written 2026-09-15, links refreshed 2026-09-16. A mechanism figure in the grammar of the draft's Figure 1, restricted to what the Exp8, Exp9 and Exp10 records measured. Nodes carry the median case; letters point to the evidence below. The small tag after each number names the event set it was measured on.

Plan. How the one-sided surge results become one page along this chart: Plan: wrapping up the one-sided surges (2026-09-23, status 2026-09-24).

The flow-shock chart

The whole event, left to right in seven stages: the flow shock, the trade (who absorbs), the inventory shock (who is shocked), the MM action, the MM outcome, the market outcome and the price outcome. The three lanes are the players: the market and its takers, the market makers, and other participants. The pill under each stage gives its time window and its tag: S state before the shock, A MM action, O MM outcome, C consequence for the market. The orange arrows are the main chain, solid arrows are links the project measures, and dashed arrows are links still to test. The badges quote medians over the 922 one-sided surges. Five stages, left to right: the state before the event, the one-sided taker surge and the maker fills it causes, the MM's inventory and balance-sheet update, its quoting, cancelling and trading, and the consequences for the market and the MM. Evidence behind each node

  • aTaker-flow surge915 events; a few very large taker orders carry the surge
    • Event of record: 30-minute bins with |zNET| ≥ 3.70 (full-sample top 5%), merged; 915 events on 345 dates. Median $23.1m taker USD, $8.7m net, 3.9x the coin's own trailing 6-hour rate. Forced flow (liquidations and ADL) is at least 10% of taker USD in 4.5% of events.
    • Length is measured on a different event set: the 922 one-sided side surges, on their 1-minute net-flow runs. The run lasts a median 14 minutes and starts 8 minutes into the 30-minute bin. The pressure peak comes 4 minutes after the sharpest minute.
    • At tick level (two episodes): one $9.29m sell carried 26% of the hit-side USD of 2025-08-11; fourteen buys of $1.4m to $1.5m from one wallet carried 79% of 2025-08-22. Orders of $1m or more carried 36% and 73%.

    Live: Exp8 §3, Exp10 §2. Surge length: Exp8 §9.3. Record: Exp8 analysis §25, §41.3; Exp10 analysis §8.2.

  • bPrice moves with the flow145 bps median, same direction in 97.7%, mostly holds
    • Start-to-end mid move a median 145 bps with a 220 bps range; direction agrees with the net flow in 97.7% of the 915 events.
    • One-sided side-surge events clearing the big-move gate (492 of 922): 68% of the peak move is still there 15 minutes after the surge ends. Patterns: flat 46%, held 22%, partly reverted 24%, reverted 7%, against the flow 0.2%.
    • Same 922 events: the price peak coincides with the pressure peak. The median offset is 0 minutes, and the price peak comes after it in 49% of events.

    Live: Exp8 §3.3. Price retention: Exp8 §9.4. Record: Exp8 analysis §25, §41.4.

  • cMMs absorb, broadly67% of hit-side flow, 118 of 135 active MMs
    • Population: every active MM per event (a post-only fill in the prior 2 hours and a fill inside the window), 119,459 event x MM rows over 914 events. Median event: 127 active MMs, 115 absorbing.
    • Labelled MMs fill a median 67% of the hit-side taker USD (roster 19%, other MMs 46%); makers with no MM label fill 33%. On 2025-10-10 21:00 the unlabelled share is 87%.
    • At tick level the MM orders that were hit had rested 1 to 14 seconds; the non-MM orders hit had rested a median 37 hours.

    Live: Exp8 §4.2, Exp10 §2. Record: Exp8 analysis §26, §34; Exp10 analysis §8.2.

  • dInventory lands on a few large, busy MMstop 5 absorbers 47% of MM absorption; shocked MMs are 6.8x larger, 4.5x busier
    • Concentration: the top 5 absorbers take a median 30% of hit-side taker flow and 47% of MM absorption; effective number of absorbers 15.6; largest single share 15%.
    • Shocked (end-of-window change ≥ 3 own sigma): 5 of 127 active MMs per event under the post-only-fill active rule. Shocked vs unshocked medians: |position| 1,753 vs 253 HYPE, submits plus cancels per bin 4.5x, share of event absorption 18x, maker fill distance to mid 2.99 vs 2.34 bps. Closeness to mid does not separate them; size and activity do.
    • The biggest absorbers are usually not the shocked ones: in 38.6% of events none of the top 5 is flagged. 74% of unflagged top-5 rows never reach 3 sigma at any minute; their peak move sits at the event's 97th percentile in HYPE but at 2.2 in their own sigma. A peak-excursion rule would flag 43.6% of them instead of 23.6%.
    • Across MMs, shock frequency is unrelated to trading activity (Spearman 0.05 with lifetime trades per bin); the correlation with position size (0.63) is largely the $12,500 scale floor binding on small wallets.
    • Two routes to being a big absorber at tick level: quote closer (roster MMs at 2 to 3 bps against peers at 5) or quote deeper (non-roster ladders 10 to 200x the peer depth).

    Live: Exp8 §4.3, §5.4, Exp10 §1. Biggest absorbers against the flagged wallets: Exp8 §8. Record: Exp8 analysis §26, §27, §34, §39, §40; Exp10 analysis §2.

  • eThe accumulating quote backs off+1.4 bps on the hit side, −0.2 bps on the exit side, never at the touch
    • 181 roster shocks, 30 minutes after the peak against the 2 hours before: best quote on the accumulating side moves from 3.25 to 5.88 bps (+1.36 bps, 81% of shocks); the exit-side best quote moves −0.19 bps (60%). The exit quote is never at or inside the touch in 178 of 181 shocks. Accumulating-side depth thins from $222k to $108k and is back at $194k within 2 hours; quoting stays continuous (presence 1.00) with turnover 1.4x faster.
    • 7,492 shocked wallet-events, distance to the market's best same-side price: over the whole 2-hour unwind the exit-side share of resting size is 0.50 and the tilt toward the touch 0.4 bps. While 75% or more of the excursion is still held, fast unwinders sit at 0.60 size share and +3.3 bps, slow ones at 0.50 and 0.0. The lean fades as the position returns.
    • Order activity runs 1.48x the wallet's own baseline during the event and 1.10x after.

    Live: Exp8 §10 (181 shocks), Exp8 §12 (tilt to the touch), Exp10 §6. Also Exp8 §4.4.

  • fUnwind, passivepost-only, half back in 150 min; speed is a MM type
    • 7,404 shocked wallet-events: unwind taker share 0, post-only (ALO) share 1.00; median 150 minutes to give back half, 36% not within 6 hours. Among the 25,705 absorbers that accumulated at least one own sigma, early unwinders (half given back by the price extreme, 21%) are not more aggressive: the median unwind taker share is zero in every timing group, and late unwinders earn the better maker markouts (M30 +4.2 against +2.2 bps).
    • Population detector, 812 shocks on 88 of 148 MMs: 60% of the excursion is back a median 30 minutes after the wallet's own peak; fast (≤ 30 min) 54%, medium 22%, slow or held 24%. Shock frequency does not order speed (Spearman −0.04).
    • Exit-side quoting orders speed across MMs (Spearman −0.46 for size share and submit share, +0.49 for exit distance to the touch) but not within an MM (−0.09). A quarter of slow rows post no exit-side order in 2 hours: they are one-sided quoters.
    • Tick level: X05 flattens in 4 minutes by taking (91% of the give-back is its own taker trades); X01 and X04 flatten in 4 to 7 minutes passively through a two-sided ladder; MM02 pulls its whole hit side and skews 30 to 37 bps yet needs 51 and 16 minutes. The same taker order fills both types in the same second.

    Live: Exp8 §4.4, Exp10 §3, Exp10 §6. Early and late unwinders: Exp8 §4.4. Record: Exp8 analysis §26, §34; Exp9 analysis §12, §13; Exp10 analysis §8.3.

  • gThe other side comes back74% of surges end by a flip; other MMs are a coin flip
    • 922 one-sided side surges, 1-minute runs: 74.0% end because in-direction net flow is zero or negative in the minute after the run (the opposite side takes over). 19.7% end because net flow stays positive but falls below the exit bar, and 6.3% at the search-window edge. On the 330 net-flow events no side surge touches, the flip share is 68.2%.
    • In the 2 hours after the event the unshocked active MMs' net flow takes the other side of the shocked MMs' unwind in 45% of 852 events, indistinguishable from a coin flip. On exact taker-maker pairs a median 14.9% of the unwind volume has a labelled MM as counterparty and 0.58% a MM shocked in the same event.

    Live: Exp8 §4.7. Flip share: Exp8 §9.3. Record: Exp8 analysis §26, §34, §41.3.

  • hMM equity: small changemedian +0.15%; the risk-limit channel is not observed
    • Equity change over the event for shocked wallet-events: median +0.14% at the event end and +0.15% six hours later; 24% below −1% and 8% below −5% at the end, 9.5% below −5% at six hours. Median pre-event account equity $732k.
    • Leverage and margin use are not measured on the 915 events. The earlier roster study (Exp8 report of 2026-09-04) carries effective leverage around the 181 shocks.
    • No test in the records links an equity drop to a subsequent quote change; the accumulating-side back-off in (e) appears in shocks with no equity loss as well.

    Live: Exp8 §4.6. Record: Exp8 analysis §26, §34.
