Market-maker inventory events on HYPE

Descriptive studies of how HYPE market makers on Hyperliquid absorb and unwind a burst of one-directional taker flow, measured against the whole labelled market-maker population, 2025-03-27 to 2026-06-30 UTC. Start with the Exp8 page; the storyline page is the same evidence read as one chain. The ⌂ icon at the top left of any page comes back here.

Each page carries its own limits section and is a reading of an analysis.md that is the authoritative record.
