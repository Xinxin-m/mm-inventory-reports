Market-maker inventory events on HYPE

Two descriptive studies of how ten top-volume Hyperliquid HYPE market makers change inventory around sudden events, measured against the whole labeled market-maker population. 2025-03-27 to 2026-06-30 UTC.

Six tables, three figures.
How to read these. Everything here is a descriptive association measured on a five-minute grid (one minute for the order-stream section) — no causal claim is made or implied. Events are detected on ten wallets only, so counts and rates describe those ten; every cross-sectional comparison is against the full labeled market-maker population. Detector precision is 92–98%, established by reading every detected panel and recording one verdict each, and the residual failure modes are listed rather than tuned away. The report carries its own limits section and is a reading of an analysis.md that is the authoritative record.

Numbers that changed during review, and why, are kept in the reports rather than quietly corrected: the report records a retracted absorption-versus-retention comparison and the reason, and the quoting section records two pilot readings that the full run reversed.

Working research output, not a paper. Wallet identities are never published: MMs are referred to only by a volume rank. Built from report.html, report_unwind_activity.html, code/site_status.html and code/site_briefing.html by make_public_site.py.
