Market-maker inventory events on HYPE

Two descriptive studies of how ten top-volume Hyperliquid HYPE market makers change inventory around sudden events, measured against the whole labeled market-maker population. 2025-03-27 to 2026-06-30 UTC.

How to read these. Everything here is a descriptive association measured on a five-minute grid — no causal claim is made or implied. Events are detected on ten wallets only, so counts and rates describe those ten; every cross-sectional comparison is against the full labeled market-maker population. Detector precision is 92–98%, established by reading every detected panel and recording one verdict each, and the residual failure modes are listed rather than tuned away. Each report carries its own limits section, and both are readings of an analysis.md that is the authoritative record.

Numbers that changed during review, and why, are kept in the reports rather than quietly corrected: Exp8 §D1 records a retracted “roughly twice the share” claim and the window defect behind it, and the Exp5 half records a retracted markout reading that came from a censored sample.

Working research output, not a paper. Wallet identities are never published: MMs are referred to only by a volume rank. Built from report.html and presentation_brief.html by make_public_site.py.
