How to read these. Everything here is a descriptive association — no causal claim is made or implied. The two studies use different event definitions and should not be pooled: the 2026-09-04 report detects events on ten wallets and its counts describe those ten, while the redefinition page detects events on market-wide taker flow and reads the whole labelled MM population against them. Cross-sectional comparisons are always against the full labelled market-maker population. Detector precision on the wallet-inventory detector is 92–98%, established by reading every detected panel and recording one verdict each, and the residual failure modes are listed rather than tuned away. Each page carries its own limits section and is a reading of an analysis.md that is the authoritative record.

Numbers that changed during review, and why, are kept in the reports rather than quietly corrected: the report records a retracted absorption-versus-retention comparison and the reason, the quoting section records two pilot readings that the full run reversed, and the redefinition page keeps the weekly-relative price gate as a labelled sensitivity beside the absolute one it replaced.

Working research output, not a paper. Wallet identities are never published: MMs are referred to only by a volume rank. Built from report.html, report_inventory_shock_09-08.html, report_unwind_activity.html, code/site_status.html and code/site_briefing.html by make_public_site.py.
