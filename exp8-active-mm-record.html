65. Who counts as an active MM, and what the quiet MM-labeled wallets do (D3, 2026-09-21)

Key results

  • Active MMs now need an order floor (decision D3d, 2026-09-22). A wallet-event row counts only when the wallet places at least 1 HYPE limit order per minute, either in the 2 hours before the event or during it. This applies on top of the daily MM label, the fill gate and the post-only rule. On the 915 net-flow boxes the floor keeps 84.2% of rows, 96.5% of shocked rows and 99.3% of absorbing maker USD. The median event keeps 105 MMs instead of 127 (65.9).
  • Shocked MMs are the same MMs; the comparison group changes. Across the section 4 statistics, the median absolute change is 2.6% for shocked MMs and 16.0% for unshocked MMs. The floor removes near-idle wallets from the unshocked group, so every shocked-against-unshocked result must be re-read on the new tables (65.9).
  • Clause (d) of the v5d label admits small automated traders, now labeled algo (D3g). 306 wallets reach the HYPE MM label only through clause (d) or the second pass. They hold 0.24% of ever-MM HYPE maker USD and place a median 22 HYPE orders a day. Inside an event window a (d)-labeled row places a median 4 orders, against 868 for a wallet labeled mostly by clause (c). Their 3,028 MM days are relabeled algo (65.6, 65.7, 65.8).
  • The daily MM label has no same-day activity floor. 18,029 of 106,509 MM-labeled HYPE wallet-days (16.9%) place fewer than 50 HYPE orders or collect fewer than 10 fills. Those days hold 0.16% of MM maker USD, and the event-window fill gate already removes 91% of them (65.3, 65.4).
  • The tenfold resting-size gap between the two population files was a data defect. The net-flow file of record wrote zero resting size on 30.5% of strictly shocked rows because the order store was read incompletely. With the fix the median rises from 64 to 402 HYPE, or 469 with the post-only rule. On the 512 (date, wallet) pairs both catalogues share, the two files give 801 and 802 HYPE. Quote distances were unaffected (65.2).
  • The 306 rule-(d) wallets, read one by one, are algo traders (65.10). 261 are algo, 3 are MMs, 19 are contested between MM and algo, and 21 are retail. They fill as maker about as often as true MMs (0.73 against 0.82 at the median), but they quote one side at a time: a buy and a sell go out in the same minute on 0.01 of their quoting minutes, against 0.72 for true MMs. They place 6.7 orders per hour in the market, against 1,514. A test that asks for both sides near the touch would replace clause (d): it keeps 83% of true MMs' days and 3% of the rule-(d) days.
  • Open (D3f): the order floor could replace the fill gate. On the net-flow boxes that adds 27,440 rows; 11,108 of them quote before the event and get no fill during it. The D3d population stays of record until the switch is confirmed (65.9).

The 10x gap in resting size between the two population files comes from the order-store defect fixed on 2026-09-15; the post-only active-MM rule moves the median by less than a fifth. The net-flow file of record, tables/redef2_pop_event_mm.csv, predates the fix and writes zero resting size on 30.5% of its strictly shocked rows. Rebuilt with the fix, its median accumulation-phase resting size on the absorption side rises from 64 to 402 HYPE, and on the 512 (date, wallet) pairs the two catalogues share, the two order-fixed files give 801 and 802 HYPE. Separately, the v5d MM label has no same-day HYPE activity floor. 18,029 of the 106,509 MM-labeled HYPE wallet-days (16.9%) place fewer than 50 HYPE orders or collect fewer than 10 fills. Those quiet days hold 0.16% of MM-labeled maker USD, and the event-window fill gate already removes 91% of them from the Exp8 population.

65.1 The three layers that make a wallet an active MM

A wallet enters an Exp8 population row through three filters applied in order: a daily label per coin, a fill gate on the event window, and a post-only fill rule. The fill gate does almost all the removing. It drops 43% of the labeled wallet-event rows but only 2.3% of the labeled MMs' absorbing maker USD.

Layer L0, the v5d label. For wallet u on coin HYPE and day t:

O_t  orders placed that day (oid)          A_t  post-only (ALO) orders placed (Alo)
F_t  frontend-market orders                g_t  shortest gap between two order events, seconds
W2   today, plus yesterday if yesterday was active
W5   the last 5 active days inside the trailing 14 calendar days
N14  active days in the trailing 14 calendar days
sums over W are written S_W(.)

(c)  A_t >= 2000
(a)  S_W2(A) >= 1000  and  S_W2(A) / S_W2(O) >= 0.7
(b)  A_t >= 200  and  A_t / O_t >= 0.7  and  g_t < 1  and  F_t / O_t < 0.01  and  7-day F/O < 0.02
(d)  N14 >= 5  and  S_W5(A) / S_W5(O) >= 0.8  and  mean_W5(g) <= 1  and  S_W5(F) / S_W5(O) < 0.01

second pass, over each wallet's days in order, when the first pass is not MM but the previous final label was:
     keep MM if  S_W2(A_all) >= 1000 and ratio >= 0.7,  or  A_t,all >= 2000,
                 or  S_W5(A_all) >= 1000 (2000 against an algo day) and ratio >= 0.5
     where A_all and O_all sum over all coins, HYPE included; a day with frontend share >= 0.3,
     or >= 0.2 over 7 days, is never kept

The first pass tests (c), (a), (b), (d) in that order and labels the day MM if any holds. The code also carries first-pass stay rules, but they run only under variant v5c1 and are off for v5d, so the only persistence routes are (d) and the second pass.

Layer L1, the fill gate. The wallet is labeled MM on the window's date and has at least one HYPE fill in PRE2H = [t0 - 2 h, t0) and at least one in EV = [t0, t1), and appears in the 5-minute position panel (code/redef2_pop_build.py lines 290-293).

Layer L2, the post-only rule. L1 plus at least one post-only fill in PRE2H. On a date where no wallet in the population has a post-only fill in PRE2H (2026-04-16 to 2026-04-23, when the fill table records no order type), the rule falls back to at least one maker fill (code/review0915_pop_activity_rule.py, rule R1; report section 4.1).

Two further columns flag rows without removing them. active_strict marks wallets with fills in at least half of the 24 five-minute PRE2H bins. order_state_complete, in the order-fixed builds, marks rows whose order-state columns can be measured; the rest carry missing values there.

  • Quiet days come from the clauses that read history or other coins: (d), (a) and the second pass.

Setup: populations, sources and definitions

Wallets. The 3,130 wallets MM-labeled on HYPE on at least one day, 2025-03-27 to 2026-06-30 (section 65.3). Each wallet's MM-labeled days carry a label route: the first of (c), (a), (b), (d) that holds, else the second pass. A wallet's cohort follows from its routes:

  • (d)-only: every MM-labeled day comes from (d) or the second pass; never (c), (a) or (b).
  • (d)-mostly: at least half its MM-labeled days come from (d), and at least one from (c), (a) or (b).
  • (c)-mostly: at least half come from (c).
  • other: the rest.

Lifetime sources. HYPE orders, post-only orders, cancels, fills and USD volume per wallet come from outputs_data/user_coin/rollup/hype_rollup_0327_0630.parquet; its order count equals the sum of the label table's daily oid on all 3,130 wallets. Daily position and markouts come from data/d3act0921_mm_fill_days.parquet, every HYPE day of these wallets. MM status on other coins comes from the ten other labels_v5d/tables/<coin>.parquet.

Measures.

  • Turnover over inventory, per day: HYPE traded that day divided by the largest absolute HYPE position that day (coins over coins). A value of 2 means the wallet bought and sold its largest position once; a market maker quoting both sides trades many times its position. Reported per wallet as the median over its active HYPE days.
  • Daily one-way share: |HYPE bought − HYPE sold| / HYPE traded, per day. 0 means buys and sells balance within the day; 1 means one direction only. Reported per wallet as the median over its active days.
  • Maker M60, bps: 10,000 × Σ 60-minute signed dollar markout of maker fills / Σ maker USD, over days with markouts; positive favours the wallet.
  • Two-sided minute share, on a sample day: of the minutes in which the wallet placed at least one HYPE limit order, the share in which it placed both a bid and an ask. The sample is every 8th MM-labeled date (58 dates, as in section 65.3), orders from the HYPE order store, rejected orders dropped.
  • Orders in a window: distinct HYPE limit orders placed in the window (<w>_orders_submitted of the order-fixed population parts), rejected excluded. Rate = orders / window minutes: EV has its own length (median 30 minutes, p95 90), PRE30 is 30 minutes, PRE2H is 120.

What clause (d) and "fast" test, exactly. From compute_labels_v5d.py lines 419-427 and the stats feed:

  • (d) holds when the wallet was active on HYPE on at least 5 of the trailing 14 calendar days, and over its last 5 active days: post-only orders are at least 80% of orders, the mean of each day's shortest order gap is at most 1 second, and frontend orders are under 1%. There is no minimum number of orders.
  • The shortest order gap, min_order_gap, is the shortest time between two consecutive order events of the wallet on HYPE that day. Recomputed from the order store as the shortest gap between consecutive order-status rows, it matches the stored value on 45 of 60 sampled wallet-days; the builder, build_user_stats.py, is not on disk.
  • "Fast" in (b) is that day's shortest gap under 1 second; in (d), the five-day mean of the daily shortest gaps at most 1 second. One pair of order events in the same second per day passes. On (d)-route days, 56% have a shortest gap of exactly 0 (two order events in the same instant) and 97% have one of at most 1 second.
  • The stats feed leaves the gap blank on any day with more than 2,000 post-only orders (SKIP_HEAVY_ALO_THRESHOLD), and the labeler fills a blank or undefined gap with 86,400 seconds. One such day among the last five makes the five-day mean at least 17,280 seconds, so (d) can fire only for wallets whose last five HYPE days all stayed at or under 2,000 post-only orders and had at least two order events. (d) is by construction a clause for small wallets: it labels 13,233 days of 1,022 wallets, with a median 58 HYPE orders and 13 fills that day, and 45% of those days place under 50 orders.

The second pass, exactly (second_pass(), lines 718-826). It walks each wallet's HYPE-active days in date order, starting from the first-pass labels:

  • The first HYPE day keeps its first-pass label.
  • A day whose HYPE frontend share is at least 30% that day, or at least 20% over the last 7 active days, becomes retail.
  • A day whose first-pass label equals the previous HYPE day's final label keeps it.
  • When the previous HYPE day ended MM and today's first pass says retail, today stays MM if any of: post-only orders on all coins over the wallet's last 2 active days (any coin) at least 1,000 with a post-only share of at least 70%; post-only orders on all coins today at least 2,000; post-only orders on all coins over its last 5 active days at least 1,000 with a share of at least 50%.
  • When the previous day ended MM and today's first pass says algo, the same, with the five-day branch at 2,000.
  • "Previous day" is the wallet's previous HYPE-active day, however long ago: the second pass has no calendar limit. A kept day counts as MM for the next comparison, so a run of quiet HYPE days stays MM as long as the wallet's post-only activity on other coins stays high.
  • "All coins" is the cross-coin feed data_enriched/all_coins/user_stats, which covers every venue coin before 2025-11-25 and the ten-coin union after.

65.7 Algorithmic or frontend? The other days of the rule-(d) wallets, and which label they should carry

Standalone. Asked on 2026-09-22, after section 65.6 found that the wallets clause (d) labels MM are small post-only traders doing about one round trip a day: are they closer to algorithmic wallets or to frontend wallets, how are their other HYPE days labeled, and which label fits them. Contract plans/plan-2026-09-22-rule-d-wallets-algo-or-frontend.md. Generated from the saved tables by code/d3e0922_section.py; edit that file and regenerate rather than editing here.

The 306 (d)-only wallets are small automated traders. They send orders the way algorithmic wallets do, and they run part-time like frontend wallets. Frontend market orders are 0.03% of their HYPE orders at the median, against 38% for frontend-labeled wallets that place as many orders on as many days. The median (d)-only wallet places its post-only orders 3.4 bps behind the traded price and keeps each for 7.8 s, and 58% of them work orders this way; matched frontend and algo wallets place orders 74 and 80 bps away that rest 4.8 min and 14.1 min. Most of their other HYPE days carry retail by default, the label the cascade gives when no clause holds: of 13,661 labeled HYPE days, 22% are MM, 50% retail by default, 11% retail for low activity, 9% retail through a frontend clause and 8% algo. On those default days they still send mostly post-only orders, only fewer of them. Algo is the label that fits: about half of the v5d algo class are passive programs resting GTC orders, and these wallets are a smaller, post-only version of them.

Setup: groups, label classes and measures

Cohorts. (d)-only, (d)-mostly and (c)-mostly as in section 65.6: from the routes of each wallet's MM-labeled HYPE days, 2025-03-27 to 2026-06-30.

Label classes of a HYPE day. From label_v5d and the first-pass triggers stored on each row of labels_v5d/tables/hype.parquet:

  • MM: label_v5d == 0. Algo: label_v5d == 2; this includes days the second pass carries from algo (233 of the (d)-only wallets' 1,031 algo days).
  • Retail (label_v5d == 1) splits by the clause behind it:
  • frontend clause: retail (a), (b) or (c) fired. (a) frontend market orders are at least 30% of the day's orders; (b) at least 20% over the last 7 active days; (c) at least 10% over the last 2 active days with at most 50 orders a day.
  • low activity: retail (d) fired: active on at least 3 of the last 14 days with fewer than 30 orders over them.
  • default: no retail clause fired and the algo vote failed. The cascade labels a day retail when nothing else holds.

Benchmark groups. HYPE wallets never MM-labeled on HYPE:

  • algo-labeled: at least half of the wallet's labeled HYPE days are algo.
  • frontend-labeled: at least half are retail through a frontend clause.
  • retail, no frontend orders: at least half are retail, and the wallet has no frontend market order on any of the ten coins with a rollup.

Measures, per wallet over its HYPE life.

  • Frontend share: frontend market orders over all HYPE orders (n_frontend_market / n_orders of the HYPE rollup). The frontend tags market orders only; a limit order placed through the frontend carries no tag.
  • TP/SL share: take-profit and stop-loss orders over all orders (n_tpsl / n_orders).
  • Same-instant days: share of the wallet's labeled days whose shortest gap between two order events is exactly 0 (min_order_gap == 0). Clause (d) reads this gap, so the measure is partly selected.
  • Maker share: maker USD over all USD volume. Cancelled share: cancelled orders over orders.
  • Turnover over position and one-way share: as in section 65.6, the median over the wallet's days in the fill sample, every 8th date (58 dates). On this sample the (d)-only median turnover is 1.5, against 1.8 over all days in section 65.6.
  • Active hours per day: median over labeled days of active_hours, the UTC hours with an order event.
  • Busiest-8-hour share H8: summing the wallet's order-active (day, hour) cells over its labeled days into 24 hour-of-day counts ch, H_8 = max over k of Σ over j = 0..7 of c_(k+j) mod 24 / Σ over h of c_h. It is 0.33 when activity spreads evenly around the clock and 1 when it fits inside 8 hours.

Order measures, on the 58 sample dates (code/redef2_pop_orders.py lifecycle rule: placement to cancel or fill, rejected and unfinished orders dropped). Every (d)-only and (d)-mostly wallet, 60 (c)-mostly wallets and 400 of each matched benchmark group, drawn with a fixed seed; 284 (d)-only wallets place orders on a sample date.

  • Distance at placement, bps: 10⁴ (P_ref - p)/P_ref for a bid and 10⁴ (p - P_ref)/P_ref for an ask, with p the limit price and P_ref the size-weighted price of all HYPE fills in the minute of placement (the last minute with fills when none). Positive means behind the price on the order's own side.
  • Order life: seconds from placement to cancel or fill. Cancelled within 5 s: share of orders cancelled at most 5 seconds after placement.
  • Price on a $0.10 grid: share of limit prices that are a multiple of $0.10; about 1% by chance at HYPE's $0.001 tick.
  • Order style of a wallet: near the touch, short-lived when its median distance is at most 10 bps and its median order life at most 60 s; far from the touch when its median distance exceeds 50 bps; in between otherwise.

Setup: the replay, the rule variants, and the all-coin day classes

Replay. Every row of labels_v5d/tables/hype.parquet (3,234,960 HYPE wallet-days) stores the first-pass triggers and the cross-coin features the second pass reads. The first pass is rebuilt from the triggers (MM clause, then retail clause, then the algo vote, else retail) and passed to second_pass() imported from labels_v5d/compute_labels_v5d.py. The replay reproduces label_v5d_firstpass on 100.00% of rows, label_v5d on 100.00%, and the stored five-day gap mean on 100.00%. HYPE only; clause (d) acts the same way on every coin.

Variants. With D the published clause (d) (active on at least 5 of the last 14 days, post-only share at least 0.8 and frontend share under 0.01 over the last 5 active days, mean shortest order gap at most 1 s over those days) and A5 the mean post-only orders per day over the last 5 active days:

  • V0 published: D labels MM.
  • V1 delete (d): D removed; those days fall through the cascade.
  • V2 (d) labels algo: D moves from the MM clauses to the algo clauses, after the retail clauses.
  • V3: D and A_5 ≥ 200 labels MM; D and A_5 < 200 labels algo. 200 is the post-only floor of clause (b).
  • V4: V3 with the blank shortest gap of a day above 2,000 post-only orders counted as fast (0 s), where the published feed fills it with 86,400 s.

The all-coin day. From data_enriched/all_coins/user_stats/user_stats_<date>.csv, one row per wallet-day summed over every coin it trades: orders, post-only orders P, frontend market orders F, maker and taker volume, n_coins and coins_traded. The feed covers every venue coin through 2025-11-24 and the ten tracked coins after, so coin counts use the days through 2025-11-24. Each wallet-day gets one class, with O the day's orders:

  • frontend: F/O ≥ 0.3 (the threshold of retail clause (a)).
  • MM-like: P ≥ 2,000, or P ≥ 200 and P/O ≥ 0.7 (the same-day MM clauses on all coins together).
  • algo-like: O ≥ 100 and F/O < 0.02.
  • small post-only: P/O ≥ 0.8, below the MM-like counts.
  • light: the rest, mostly days with fewer than 100 orders and a post-only share under 0.8.

A wallet is "MM most days" when at least half its days are MM-like; a "switcher" when at least 10% of its days are frontend and at least 10% are MM-like or algo-like; "frontend most days" at half frontend; "automated, below MM scale" when algo-like and small post-only days make at least half; "mixed or light" otherwise.

The rule of record. A (window x wallet) row is in the population when all hold:

  • at least one post-only fill in PRE2H (post-only rule);

  • a readable order store for the row (order_state_complete). The 234 rows that fail it cannot be tested; they are listed in tables/d3d0922_unreadable_rows.csv and not kept.

Every kept row carries d3d_active_before and d3d_active_during. A row active before and not during is an MM that slowed its quoting during the event. It stays in the population, so quote withdrawal remains measurable.

Algo: all 142 matched wallets; frontend and retail no-FM: random draws. - Algo, all (300). Drawn at random from every algo-labeled wallet, with no activity match.

v5d MM clauses (per HYPE day; the first that holds, in the order (c), (a), (b), (d)). - (c): 2,000+ post-only orders that day. - (a): 1,000+ post-only orders over today and yesterday, 70%+ of orders. - (b): 200+ post-only orders today, 70%+ of orders, shortest order gap under 1 s, frontend under 1%. - (d): active on 5 of the last 14 days; over the last 5 active days post-only 80%+ of orders, mean daily shortest gap 1 s or less, frontend under 1%. No minimum number of orders. - Second pass: a day the first pass does not call MM stays MM because the previous HYPE day was MM and the wallet's post-only activity on all coins stays high (section 65.6).

BTC orders exist only from 2025-09-27. - Orders, all coins: data_enriched/all_coins/user_stats/user_stats_{date}.csv, one row per wallet per day. Measures (one value per wallet; ten coins merged unless the row says otherwise). - Order gap: seconds between consecutive orders of the wallet within a UTC day, coins merged. Each wallet gets its own p25, p50, p75, p95; the tables give quantiles of those across wallets. A wallet's p95 includes the breaks between its sessions. - Orders per active day: orders over days with at least one order. - Hours in the market: UTC hours with an order placed or a limit order resting; median over active days. - Run of active hours: consecutive hours in the market on the calendar; a run can cross midnight. - Orders per hour in the market: orders over hours in the market. - Two-sided resting share: of the time a limit order rests on a coin, the share with a bid and an ask resting at once. - Two-sided minute share: coin-minutes with both a buy and a sell limit order placed, over coin-minutes with any limit order placed. - Quote distance: limit price against the mid at placement, bps; median over the orders of the wallet's main limit type (post-only or GTC). - Near the touch: a quote distance of 30 bps or less. - Order-type shares: each order has one time in force: post-only (Alo), GTC, IOC or frontend market. TP/SL is a flag on top. - One-way share: on a day with fills, |bought - sold| / (bought + sold) in coin units; median over the wallet's coin-days. 0 means it bought as much as it sold; 1 means one direction only. - Turnover: coin traded that day over the day's largest absolute position; median over coin-days. - Round trip: opening a position and closing it. Example: buy 100 HYPE and sell 100 HYPE with a largest position of 100 gives one-way share 0 and turnover 2, one round trip. Turnover 4 or more is two or more round trips. - Maker share: maker USD over all USD filled.

Day classes (one per wallet-day, ten coins merged). - Light: under 5 orders. - Frontend: frontend market plus TP/SL orders are 30% or more of the day's orders. - Program: frontend plus TP/SL under 10%, and either half the day's gaps under 10 s or post-only orders living a median under 10 s. - Program, two sides: a program day with bid and ask resting together half the resting time or more. - Program, one side: any other program day. - Slow: the rest. - Mixed day: a frontend order inside an otherwise fast program day, or a frontend day on one coin and a program day on another the same date. - Switcher: 3+ frontend days and 3+ program days, each 10% or more of the wallet's non-light days.

The rule (new in this section; first match wins). - Too few: under 50 orders over the window. - Program: half or more of the non-light days are program days; or frontend plus TP/SL under 10% of orders and any of: gap p50 of 10 s or less; post-only life p50 of 10 s or less (50+ post-only orders); orders in 12+ UTC hours on the median day; 500+ orders per active day. - MM coin: a coin with 50+ orders from the wallet, its quotes near the touch, and two-sided resting share 0.5+ or two-sided minute share 0.2+. - MM: a program with 25% or more of its USD volume on MM coins. - Algo: any other program. - Retail: not a program, and frontend plus TP/SL 10% or more of orders, or frontend days 30% or more of non-light days. - Unclear: the rest (slow, few frontend orders). - Where each threshold comes from: - 12+ hours and 500+ orders a day: relabeling analysis 8.5, bot signals B2 and B3 (Allium/experiments/relabeling/analysis.md). - Two-sided minute share 0.2: relabeling 8.5, market-maker test. - Frontend 30%: v5d retail clause (a). - 10 s gap, 10 s post-only life, 10% frontend, 0.5 resting share, 25% of USD, 50 orders: chosen in this section. - 30 bps: chosen after the blind read. Against the readers' verdicts it gave 15 wallets called MM that the readers did not; a "closest quarter within 20 bps" version gave 23. The MM class is therefore tuned on the readers.

Blind read. - Four agents each read about 107 of 426 wallets: the 306 (d)-only wallets plus 120 from the other groups, shuffled. - Each wallet is one row of measures (those of Tables 34 and 35, the day-class counts and the top three coins), without its address, group or v5d label. The brief, plans/d3g0923_blind_reader_brief.md, describes an MM, an algo and a retail wallet in words. - Each reader gave a verdict (MM, algo, retail or unclear), a confidence (h, m, l), switcher and part-time flags, and a reason. - The read is not fully independent of the rule: the readers saw the rule's day-class counts, and the brief's MM (both sides, near the best prices) is the rule's MM.

Final label (tables/d3g0923_donly_final.csv). - Rule and readers agree: that class. - Rule unclear: the readers' class (MM, contested when no coin is an MM coin). - Rule retail only because of TP/SL, readers algo: algo (the TP/SL orders come through the API). - One of the two says MM: MM, contested.

Validation. - The 323 wallets first active on or after 2025-09-27 reproduce the relabeling wallet summaries exactly on order counts and gap p25, p50 and p75. HYPE fill USD and maker USD equal the rollups to 1e-7. - Daily net fills equal the position change on 99.96% of rows from 2025-05-26. - The all-coin fill pass reproduces the ten-coin fill USD within 1% for 92% of wallets (pooled ratio 1.003); the rest are the two days the ten-coin fill source is short. - The all-coin order counts equal the ten-coin pass exactly on 39% of wallet-days that trade only the ten coins (median ratio 0.94): user_stats counts orders with any event that day, including orders placed the day before.
